Work Related Education

A 7-unit, fixed-sequence programme where each unit builds something the next one uses: career direction first, then self-awareness, a CV before the job application, interview practice straight after it, a work placement, and a career portfolio that pulls it all together. Four award sizes are banked along the way.

i

About this Programme

Covers career exploration, personal confidence and self-awareness, CV writing, job applications, interview skills, a work placement and a personal career portfolio. Units are taught in a fixed order because each one produces material a later unit relies on. Full order, GLH and award checkpoints are on the Scheme of Work page.

One nested unit set — each smaller award is a safety net banked on the way to the Extended Certificate, not a separate teaching plan. This programme delivers 13 Level Two + 6 Level One = 19 credits.

F

Digital Workbook Format

Each unit is delivered as a self-contained HTML digital workbook (works offline, no installation), a printable DOCX version for handwriting, and a PPTX teaching deck. Digital workbooks have a learner mode and a password-gated assessor mode with Met / Not-yet judgements per assessment criterion.

The assessor password is shared with staff directly and is not stored in this repository.

A

Assessment

All units are internally assessed by the teacher (assessor) and internally verified by the centre. There are no exams. Evidence comes from written tasks, structured lists, reflection, a real CV and job application, interview role play or simulation, and work placement evidence.

Unit Resources

Units are grouped by the award each stage completes. Click a unit to see its resources. New files appear automatically once uploaded to the repository with the unit code anywhere in the filename. For teaching order and checkpoints, see the Scheme of Work.
