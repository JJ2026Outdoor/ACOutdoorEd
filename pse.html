About this Programme

Delivered as a 6th-form Life Skills programme, this suite covers emotional regulation, assertiveness, decision-making, personal safety, money management, problem-solving and online safety. Units are taught in a fixed order so each Level 2 unit extends the vocabulary and concepts of a specific Level 1 unit taught earlier in the year.

Same suite — the Certificate is a safety net, not a separate teaching plan. Max 6 L1 credits count toward the Certificate (7 toward the Extended Certificate); min 7 L2 credits required for the Certificate (10 for the Extended Certificate). This programme delivers 7 L1 + 11 L2 = 18 credits total.

F

Digital Workbook Format

Each unit is delivered as a self-contained HTML digital workbook (works offline, no installation), a printable DOCX version for handwriting, and a PPTX teaching deck. Digital workbooks have a learner mode and a password-gated assessor mode with Met / Not-yet judgements per assessment criterion.

The assessor password is shared with staff directly and is not stored in this repository.

A

Assessment

All units are internally assessed by the teacher (assessor) and internally verified by the centre. There are no exams. Evidence is gathered through written tasks, structured lists, personal reflection, and — where relevant to a unit's assessment method — voice recording or practical demonstration.

Programme total: 143 GLH against confirmed unit specifications — 26 hours under Agored's published 169 GLH figure for the suite. At 4 hours/week this is roughly 36 sessions, leaving 9–13 hours of contingency for absence, resits, moderation and portfolio catch-up across a 38–39 week year.

Foundation → Progression Pairings

Each Level 2 unit sits after the Level 1 unit whose vocabulary and concepts it extends.

Understanding Stress (Unit 8) extends Anger Management (Unit 1)
Emotional Intelligence (Unit 9) extends Assertiveness Skills (Unit 2)
Personal Money Management (Unit 10) extends Decision-Making (Unit 3)
Problem-Solving Skills (Unit 11) extends Decision-Making (Unit 3)
Anti-bullying Awareness (Unit 12) extends Negotiation Skills (Unit 4)
Staying Safe Online (Unit 13) extends Fire Safety in the Home (Unit 6)
Crime Prevention and Keeping Safe (Unit 14) extends Social/Legal Aspects of Personal Safety (Unit 7)
Healthy Lifestyles (Unit 5) has no Level 2 partner in this programme — a deliberate, agreed gap.
