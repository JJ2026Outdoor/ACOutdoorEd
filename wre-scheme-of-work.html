Check against Agored's registration paperwork the first time a learner is entered. No barred combinations: each unit title is used at one level only.

Why this order

  • Career Exploration first — sets a career direction everything else is built around.
  • Personal Confidence and Self Awareness second — the self-awareness work feeds every later reflective task (CV strengths, interview self-assessment, portfolio goals).
  • CV before Applying for a Job — the application needs a CV to attach.
  • Interview Skills straight after Applying for a Job — both cover interviews from different angles; back-to-back reinforces the skill. Not a barred pair, so both count.
  • Work Experience before the Portfolio — gives real placement evidence to include.
  • Career Portfolio last — it assembles the CV, achievement evidence and a goals action plan built on Units 1 and 2. A genuine capstone.
